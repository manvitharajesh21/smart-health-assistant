Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy balanced diet?

A balanced diet includes fruits, vegetables, whole grains, protein sources and adequate water. Individual nutritional needs may differ.

How can I maintain good hygiene?

Wash hands regularly, maintain oral hygiene, keep food clean and maintain a clean living environment.

Why is drinking water important?

Water supports normal body functions, temperature regulation and digestion. Fluid needs vary depending on activity, climate and health.

Why is sleep important?

Sleep supports concentration, learning, mood and general health. Maintain a regular sleep schedule.

How can I prevent seasonal infections?

Wash hands, cover coughs and sneezes, improve ventilation and follow recommended vaccinations.

What should I do if I have a fever?

Rest, drink fluids and monitor your symptoms. Seek medical advice if fever persists, worsens or causes concern.

What is blood pressure?

Blood pressure is the force of blood against artery walls. A healthcare professional can help interpret readings.

Why is physical activity important?

Regular age-appropriate physical activity supports heart health, strength, mood and general wellbeing.

Common Health Topics

General educational information about common symptoms.

🤧 Common Cold

Symptoms: Runny nose, sneezing, sore throat and cough.

General care: Rest, drink fluids and maintain hygiene.

🤒 Fever

Symptoms: Raised body temperature, chills and tiredness.

General care: Rest, maintain hydration and monitor symptoms.

🤕 Headache

Symptoms: Pain or pressure in the head.

General care: Rest and maintain regular meals and hydration.

🌿 Allergy

Symptoms: Sneezing, itching, watery eyes or rash.

General care: Avoid known triggers and consult a healthcare professional.

🍽️ Indigestion

Symptoms: Bloating, discomfort or feeling full.

General care: Eat regular meals and seek advice if symptoms continue.

😴 Sleep Difficulties

Symptoms: Trouble falling asleep or staying asleep.

General care: Maintain a regular sleep routine.

⚠️ Important Health Warning

Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, sudden weakness, fainting or signs of stroke may indicate a medical emergency. Do not rely on this website for diagnosis. Seek emergency medical care immediately.

In India: Call 112 for emergency assistance.
